Loan Eligibility:
1. Personal loans are available to customers who meet the bank's eligibility criteria.
2. Eligibility depends on factors including age, income, employment status, credit history and required documentation.
3. Meeting the eligibility criteria does not guarantee loan approval.
4. The final approval decision is made by the bank after verification.

KYC Requirements:
1. Customers must provide valid identity proof and address proof as required by the bank.
2. The bank may request additional documents when required for verification.
3. Customers must ensure that submitted documents are valid and accurate.
4. KYC verification must be completed before applicable banking services can be provided.

Service Charges:
1. Applicable service charges depend on the banking service used and the current bank schedule of charges.
2. Customers should refer to the approved schedule of charges for the applicable fee.
3. Charges may vary depending on the type of service and account.
4. The FAQ assistant must not invent or estimate a fee when the applicable charge is not stated in the approved policy.
